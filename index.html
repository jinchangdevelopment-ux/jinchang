不動產與實體資產重整 Real Estate & Real Assets

針對處於地緣優勢但營運停滯的商業大樓、工業用地及住宅組合，進行產權理清、債務清理與再定位重組，提升資產收益率。 Targeting prime commercial structures, industrial land, and residential portfolios with operational bottlenecks to clear titles, restructure debt, and elevate yield.

企業資本重整與治理改組 Corporate Recapitalization

為具有市場潛力與重組價值的企業提供緊急債資注入，改組董事會並派駐專業團隊優化營運，恢復企業健康財務結構。 Injecting rescue capital into high-potential corporate entities facing liquidity constraints, restructuring boards, and deploying operational experts.

🌐

跨國併購與架構設計 Cross-Border M&A & Structuring

協助機構合夥人設計跨境持股信託與避險結構，在跨司法管轄區的交易中實現最優稅務配置與風險隔離。 Structuring secure cross-border holding trusts and hedging vehicles for institutional partners, ensuring tax efficiency and asset insulation during M&A.

特殊機會與不良債權收購 Special Situations & NPLs
