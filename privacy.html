個人資料保護 Privacy & Protection

晉昌開發集團 隱私權政策 Jin Chang Development Group Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 7 月 29 日 ｜ 個人資料保護與隱私防護聲明 Effective Date: July 29, 2026 | Privacy & Personal Data Protection Statement

晉昌開發集團（連同其子公司、關聯企業及管理的投資主體，以下合稱「晉昌國際」、「本機構」或「我們」）極度重視您的隱私權與資料安全。本隱私權政策旨在說明我們如何蒐集、處理、利用及保護透過本網站取得的個人與機構資料。 Jin Chang Development Group (together with its subsidiaries, affiliates, and managed investment entities, collectively referred to as "Jin Chang," "the Firm," or "we") values your privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, utilize, and protect personal and institutional data obtained through this Website.

1. 蒐集目的與適用範圍 1. Purpose of Collection & Scope

本機構為獨立的跨境私募股權與資產投資機構，不對一般公眾提供零售金融或地產仲介服務。本網站資料蒐集之目的僅限於：

• 處理與評估資產收購與股權重組提案。
• 建立與維護合格機構合夥人、境外信託與家族辦公室之投資人關係。
• 執行反洗錢（AML）、認識客戶（KYC）等法規遵循與盡職調查（Due Diligence）程序。
• 回覆線上諮詢表單及後續聯絡溝通。

As an independent cross-border private equity and asset investment firm, Jin Chang does not provide retail wealth management or real estate brokerage to the general public. Data collected through this Website is strictly used for:

• Evaluating asset acquisition and corporate restructuring proposals.
• Establishing and maintaining investor relations with qualified institutional partners, offshore trusts, and family offices.
• Executing anti-money laundering (AML), know-your-customer (KYC), and due diligence compliance procedures.
• Responding to online inquiry submissions and operational communications.

2. 蒐集的資料類別 2. Categories of Data Collected

當您填寫網站之「安全諮詢表單」或與我們聯繫時，我們會蒐集以下資訊：

• 機構資訊： 機構或企業全稱。
• 聯絡人資訊： 聯絡代表人姓名、機構公務電子信箱、公司聯絡電話等。
• 提案資訊： 提案或諮詢概述中所包含之商業計畫、財務狀況或資產細節。
• 自動蒐集資訊： IP 位址、瀏覽器類型、存取時間及 Cookie（僅用於網站基本運作與資訊安全防護，不作侵入式行銷追蹤）。

When you submit our "Secure Inquiry Form" or contact us directly, we collect:

• Institutional Data: Corporate or entity legal name.
• Contact Representative Data: Representative name, official business email address, corporate phone number.
• Proposal Information: Business plans, financial conditions, or asset details included in inquiry summaries.
• Automatically Collected Technical Data: IP address, browser type, access timestamps, and essential cookies (used solely for website operation and cybersecurity, without invasive ad-tracking).

3. 資料利用期間、地區、對象及方式 3. Data Retention, Region, Recipients & Methods

• 期間： 自您提交資料起，至蒐集目的消失、合約終止，或依相關法令（如商業會計法、洗錢防制法）所訂之保存期限為止。
• 地區： 台灣總部（台中市西區）、本機構之海外據點，以及為執行跨境資產重組所涉及之相關司法管轄區。
• 對象： 本機構內部經授權之投資、法務、財務團隊，以及為完成評估與交易所需之外部專業顧問（如律師、會計師、第三方查核機構）。
• 方式： 透過符合國際資安標準之數位加密傳輸與實體隔離措施進行資料處理，確保商業機密與個資之安全。

• Retention Period: Retained from submission until purpose fulfillment, contract termination, or statutory archiving requirements (e.g., Commercial Accounting Act, Money Laundering Control Act).
• Region: Taiwan Headquarters (Taichung City), overseas operating hubs, and relevant cross-border transaction jurisdictions.
• Authorized Recipients: Authorized internal investment, legal, and financial teams, as well as external professional advisors (lawyers, CPAs, third-party auditors) under confidentiality obligations.
• Processing Methods: Processed using international security standard digital encryption and physical insulation to safeguard corporate secrets and personal data.

4. 資料分享與第三方揭露 4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

我們堅守「資本保全與價值重塑」的保密原則。絕不出售、交換或出租您的個人或機構資料予任何無關之第三方。資料僅在以下例外情況進行分享：

• 為推進資產重組與私募注資程序，並已與第三方（如聯貸銀行、信託機構）簽署保密協議（NDA）。
• 依法令規定或受司法、監管機關之合法要求。

Adhering to our core principle of "Capital Preservation & Value Realignment," Jin Chang never sells, trades, or rents your personal or institutional data to unrelated third parties. Disclosures occur strictly under:

• Advancing asset restructuring and equity injection under signed Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) with counterparties (e.g., syndicated banks, trust institutions).
• Legal compliance or valid requests issued by judicial and regulatory authorities.

5. 當事人權利行使 5. Data Subject Rights

依據《個人資料保護法》，您可以針對您的個人資料行使以下權利：

• 查詢或請求閱覽。
• 請求製給複製本。
• 請求補充或更正。
• 請求停止蒐集、處理或利用。
• 請求刪除。

若欲行使上述權利，請發送電子郵件至機構合夥人關係聯絡信箱：jcdevsupport@jinchangdev.com，我們的合規團隊將於法定期限內為您處理。

Under applicable Personal Data Protection laws, you may exercise the following rights regarding your personal records:

• Inquire or request access.
• Request copy duplication.
• Request supplementation or correction.
• Request restriction of collection, processing, or usage.
• Request erasure or deletion.

To exercise these rights, please email our compliance team at jcdevsupport@jinchangdev.com, and your request will be processed within statutory timelines.

6. 資訊安全 6. Information Security

本網站的諮詢表單皆採用安全加密技術送出。本機構內部實施嚴格的存取控制與權限隔離，防範未經授權的存取、竄改或資料外洩。

Submissions via this Website are transmitted using secure encryption protocols. Jin Chang enforces strict internal access controls and permission isolation to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or data breaches.
